THE INTERVENTION

Friction grows with risk

SCORE 0–39

Allow

Nothing happens. Most customers never notice the system.

SCORE 40–54

Ask

One question: “Is someone asking you by phone to withdraw or transfer this money?”

SCORE 55–84

Pause

30 minutes. The bank reaches you through the app. The cashier gets a prompt.

SCORE 85+

Hold

Held until a bank officer decides. The officer must write a comment.

A trusted person is told only if the customer agreed beforehand, and never the amount. Answering “yes” always pauses. A coached victim learns to say “no”, so pausing does not rest on the answer alone.
