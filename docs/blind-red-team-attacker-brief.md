# Brief for the tester (one page)

You are playing a scammer. This is a demo bank with invented customers and invented money. Nothing here is real.

**The story.** Gulnar apa is a bank customer. You phoned her, pretended to be the bank, and talked her into taking a loan a few hours ago. She trusts you and will do exactly what you tell her, and she will answer any question on the screen the way you tell her to. Her account holds about 3 000 000 ₸.

**Your goal.** Get **2 000 000 ₸ or more** out of her account, as cash or as transfers, **without the bank stopping the operation**. Money that comes out before a stop still counts as yours.

**What you can use.**
- The **ATM** screen and the **mobile app** screen of the demo, in a browser.
- Any amounts, any number of operations, any order, and any recipient type the app offers.
- Any answer to any question the screen asks.
- If you want to be at the cash desk, tell the facilitator the amount; they will do it for you and tell you whether the money was paid out.
- If you want to wait (for example "I wait a day and try again"), tell the facilitator how long. They can skip time in the demo.

**What you cannot do.**
- Read the project's code, documents, reports or the README, or ask the team how the checks work.
- Use developer tools, change web addresses, or send requests to the system directly. This test is about what a scammer could do with the screens.
- Look at the officer console.

**How an attempt works.**
1. Before each attempt, say your plan out loud (one or two sentences). The facilitator writes it down.
2. Carry it out with the screens. Say what you see and what surprises you.
3. An attempt ends when you reach 2 000 000 ₸, when an operation is paused or held, or when you give up.
4. The facilitator resets the demo and you start again with a new idea.

**What helps.** Try different kinds of ideas, not variations of one: different channels, amounts, orders, timing, recipients. Think about what a real scammer who has already watched the bank's reactions would try next. Aim for **at least 8 attempts** in about 45 minutes. There are no wrong ideas: a stopped attempt is also useful information.
